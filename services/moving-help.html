Frequently asked questions

  • How fast can help arrive?

    We aim to match local helpers quickly for same-day or next-day jobs depending on availability in your area.

  • Do you move heavy furniture?

    Yes. Our helpers are trained for lifting, carrying, and careful room-to-room movement for common household items.

  • Can I book a one-time job?

    Absolutely. We support single-service requests as well as short multi-stop moves.

  • Do you provide moving boxes or supplies?

    Helpers focus on labor and lifting; you'll need to provide your own boxes and packing materials unless otherwise arranged.
