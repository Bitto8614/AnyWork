Frequently asked questions

  • What kind of construction projects can you help with?

    We support everything from small repairs and painting jobs to full kitchen and bathroom renovations, framing, flooring, decks, and additions.

  • Are your construction crews licensed and insured?

    Yes, all construction professionals on AnyWork365 are vetted for experience, licensing where required, and insurance coverage before they're approved to take jobs.

  • How is pricing determined for construction work?

    Pricing depends on project scope and materials. You'll receive a detailed estimate after a site review, with costs confirmed before any work begins.

  • Can you handle larger renovation projects, not just quick repairs?

    Yes, we support multi-day and multi-week projects and can coordinate with other trades, like electricians and plumbers, already on our platform.

  • Do I need to supply materials myself?

    No, our crews can source materials for you, or work with materials you've already purchased, whichever works best for your budget and timeline.
